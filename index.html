Apple Music7

Build the visual identity from a physical process applied to the brand's core product (here sound through oil), so the logo is evidence of the product and the story of how it was made becomes part of the brand.

Why it works

A new Apple Music identity by TBWA\Media Arts Lab: a sound mnemonic made with producer Fred again.. and Nest Acoustics, whose visuals were filmed from that sound played through liquid, plus a bold lowercase wordmark in custom SF Pro beside the Apple symbol.

Music is invisible, so most music brands borrow waveforms and equalizer bars that only illustrate it. Here the sound was played through a transducer into a shallow bath of mineral oil, and the waves and refractions were filmed, so the mark is a physical trace of the note itself. The order of work is the distinctive part: sound first, visuals derived from it, with a credible artist and an acoustics studio as co-authors, which gives the audio and visual assets one shared origin. That origin supports the line 'made by sound' and makes the asset hard to copy.
