Mazda Australia

'It's Kind of a Big Deal,' a national campaign by Clemenger BBDO for the redesigned Next-Gen CX-5, anchored by a film shot on Mount Wellington, Tasmania.

It takes the cliche 'sing its praises' and stages it literally: a choir of actual Mazda designers, engineers, and drivers climbs the mountain alongside the car, singing an original song about its features. The literalized idiom gives the agency a structural excuse to turn spec sheet claims into lyrics without sounding like a spec sheet, and using real employees instead of actors adds a layer of authenticity to the craft claim.

steal thisWhen a product's real news is incremental engineering upgrades, find an idiom that can be staged literally, then let the people who built the thing perform it.

The Singleton (Diageo)

A global packaging refresh across The Singleton's core single malt Scotch range, covering both The Singleton of Dufftown and The Singleton of Glendullan lines, rolling out from July 2026.

A premium packaging refresh signals category-wide pressure on single malt Scotch to look more distinctive on crowded back-bars and shelves as premiumisation becomes the main lever brands pull instead of price cuts. Unifying two sub-ranges under one bolder visual identity trades local variation for shelf recognition at global scale, a trade-off drinks brands make when travel retail and export volume matter more than regional nuance.

steal thisWhen a brand runs multiple sub-lines under one name, a packaging refresh is a cheap way to force visual coherence across them without touching the liquid or the price tier.
